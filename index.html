What is SoundSmart?

Sound turns passive displays into experiences shoppers remember

SoundSmart transforms standees, packaging and posters into high-quality interactive sound systems. A slim sound unit attaches behind the display and turns the panel itself into a flat-panel speaker.

The moment a shopper approaches - or presses a button - your message plays instantly. When they move on, it stops. No screens, no apps, no staff required. Pre-programmed, it's built for short-term in-store promotional campaigns that need to cut through a busy retail floor.

How It Works

Simple, reliable technology

No wiring, no apps, no ongoing tech costs. SoundSmart attaches to your existing display and works reliably for the length of your campaign.

01

Attach it to your display

The slim, pre-taped sound unit fixes straight onto corrugated liners, foam board or fluted plastic. No wiring and no construction required.

02

A shopper triggers it

A motion sensor detects someone nearby, or they press one of up to four buttons. Playback starts instantly - no phone, no app, no login.

03

Your display speaks

High-quality audio plays through the panel itself - up to 175 seconds of sound bites. Then silence returns until the next shopper arrives.
